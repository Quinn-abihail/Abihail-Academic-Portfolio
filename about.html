About

A data analyst who has also fixed engines, taught four-year-olds, and sold perfume.

Alongside school, I run two small businesses — a resin art studio and Phoebe's Paragon, a personal chef and personal shopping service — and I've prepared two novel manuscripts for release on AlphaNovel. Details on all of that are on the work page.
